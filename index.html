Growth Deep-Dive Takeaways

  • No channel in this set is winning with a steady flow of Thailand Shorts. The big Shorts numbers come from one-off outliers: a relatable moment, a "local secret" reveal, or a strong opinion. Generic location titles don't produce them.
  • Our median Short views relative to subs (0.84) beat almost every peer. The weak spot is the titles. "Expat in Thailand", "Expat Life in Thailand" and "Living in Chiang Mai" are interchangeable, and none of them makes a promise or creates a curiosity gap.
  • The closest peer is Sean & Pamela: a US family of 4 in Thailand with about the same subs (1,330). Their Shorts are built on a number plus a family hook ("$10 Lunch Feeds All 4 of Us", "$18 Dinner vs. $5,000 Rent"), and their median is about 2x ours (2,400 vs 1,200). They have posted no Shorts since Jun 3, which leaves that lane open.
  • Post 15 Shorts in 30 days, about 3.5 a week, close to our current pace (10 in the last 30 days). Weight the schedule to Thu–Sat, which the timing study favours.
